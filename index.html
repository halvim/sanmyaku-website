How it works

01 · READ

Understand the case

Language models are used only to interpret text: letters, forms, messages. They don't decide, and TRACE doesn't rely on what they know. Structured data needs no language model at all.

02 · DECIDE

Apply your rules

Your firm's own rules make the decision, not a model's best guess. Your expertise, entered once, used every time.

03 · PROVE

Keep the reasoning

Each decision is stored with its facts, its rule and its outcome. Years later, it reopens exactly as it was decided.
